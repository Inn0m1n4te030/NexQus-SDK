How to read this report

    Block boundaries and feature/weight roles are annotations supplied by the report author. Highlighting shows where a parameter occurs, not how important it is. This report does not calculate feature attribution or causal explanations. Basis views show at most 16 states; the downloadable trace contains every basis state. Playback follows recorded operations, not hardware timing or the critical-path schedule.
