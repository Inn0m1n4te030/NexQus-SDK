What am I looking at?

Spheres mark atomic positions; sticks show supplied or inferred chemical connectivity. Atom sizes are illustrative. Rotation changes the camera, not the molecule's coordinates. These spheres are not electron-density surfaces.

The qubit estimate uses all STO-3G spin orbitals, with no active-space or symmetry reduction. It is a basis-size estimate, not a completed quantum calculation.
